The law, written by Senator Scott Wiener and Mayor Daniel Lurie specifically for San Francisco and in effect since June, lets police act against people selling commonly stolen goods without a permit or proof of purchase. Two months in, SFPD reports 56 citations, 396 warnings, and zero misdemeanor arrests, which tracks with the stated goal of pushing illegal fencing operations out while protecting the Mission's 33 permitted vendors.
