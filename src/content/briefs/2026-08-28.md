    editor_note: |
      The city cannot be sued over the tracks that keep killing cyclists, because the tracks were designed correctly. SFMTA holds design immunity, which means infrastructure that cleared a proper approval process cannot ground a liability claim no matter what it does afterward. So the usual mechanism for forcing a fix is closed. What is left is engineering, and the engineering is not there either: the flangeway fillers being tested in Basel and Cologne are fragile, wear dangerous, and cost more than the city has put behind them. Mark Heryer died this way in 2015. Injury lawyers say they field about two calls a month. Reported crashes on Market ran 53 last year against 60 in 2019, on roughly a quarter fewer riders.
