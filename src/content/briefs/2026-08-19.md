This brief covered the Castro bars dropping facial scanners on Sunday and the hole underneath that story: San Francisco's 2019 facial recognition ban reaches city government only, so private operators sit outside it. Flock is the same gap at larger scale. The state's Private Investigator Act exempts police and insurers but not vendors who contract with police, so a license converts a legal exposure into a credential.
