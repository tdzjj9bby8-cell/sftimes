    editor_note: |
      Sacramento lowered a floor and a developer took the discount three years into a project. Builder's remedy lets a development skirt local zoning in a city that missed its housing element deadline, so long as a share of the homes are affordable. When DeNardi Wang Homes applied in 2023, that share was 20 percent. State law has since cut it to 13 percent, and days before the Mountain View hearing the developer emailed the city to say it would build to the new number.
